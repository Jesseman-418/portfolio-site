Explored how a team of reinforcement learning agents could help a classifier stay accurate under adversarial perturbations. I evaluated clean and perturbed MNIST inputs to compare the tradeoffs.

Method and scope

The prototype used PPO agents and a shared coordinator. The results apply to the experimental MNIST setup, with no claim about performance on other datasets or deployed systems.
